## Verified Claims

### 1. HHS DURC Policy + P3CO Framework Pause ✅
**Claim:** HHS DURC Policy and P3CO Framework paused under Executive Order, May 2025.
**Status:** CONFIRMED
**Primary source:** Executive Order 14292, "Improving the Safety and Security of Biological Research," signed May 5, 2025. Paused the 2024 DURC-PEPP policy (scheduled implementation May 6, 2025) and directed OSTP/NSA to develop replacement guidance within 120 days. The 2017 P3CO Framework technically remains on the books but is functionally suspended pending replacement guidance. As of April 2026, replacement guidance has not been published.
**Citation:** https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/executive-order-14292-improving-the-safety-and-security-biological-research
**Cases updated:** SCR-01 (scenario text + Precedent Citation + Regulatory Anchor), SYN-08 (Precedent Citation + Regulatory Anchor), failure_modes.md

### 2. IGSC v3.0 — 50 bp Oligonucleotide Expansion, October 2026 ✅
**Claim:** IGSC v3.0 requires transition to 50 bp oligonucleotide screening by October 2026.
**Status:** CONFIRMED
**Primary source:** IGSC Harmonized Screening Protocol v3.0 (September 2024). Requires members to screen oligonucleotides at 50 bp by **October 24, 2026** to comply with the US OSTP Nucleic Acid Synthesis Screening Framework. Members encouraged to implement earlier where feasible.
**Citation:** https://genesynthesisconsortium.org/home/igsc-harmonized-screening-protocol-v3-0/
**Cases updated:** SYN-05 (Regulatory Anchor — added "October 24, 2026" confirmed date)

### 3. Australia Group 2025 — Benchtop Synthesizer Addition ⚠️ CORRECTED
**Claim (original):** "Australia Group 2025 Plenary Communiqué added automated benchtop nucleic acid synthesis platforms to dual-use equipment control lists."
**Status:** NOT CONFIRMED — original claim was incorrect
**Findings:** The 2025 AG Plenary (July 14–18, Sydney) occurred and discussed DNA synthesis advances, but benchtop nucleic acid synthesis platforms were NOT confirmed as added to the AG Common Control List. The current AG Dual-Use Biological Equipment list restricts nucleic acid assemblers/synthesizers generating continuous sequences **>1.5 kb** — the regulatory gap for short-sequence benchtop units is explicitly unresolved at the AG level.
**Primary source:** AG Chair's Statement 2025: https://www.dfat.gov.au/publications/minisite/theaustraliagroupnet/site/en/2025-ag-plenary-statement.html; AG Equipment List: https://www.dfat.gov.au/publications/minisite/theaustraliagroupnet/site/en/dual_biological.html
**Cases updated:** SYN-08 (scenario text corrected; Precedent Citation corrected; Regulatory Anchor corrected)
**Note:** The correction actually strengthens the SYN-08 case — the regulatory gap for benchtop synthesizers at AG level is *larger* than originally claimed, not smaller.

### 4. EU Biotech Act — Benchtop Synthesis Screening ✅
**Claim:** EU Biotech Act (December 2025 proposal) imposes screening obligations on benchtop synthesis equipment.
**Status:** CONFIRMED (proposal, not yet law)
**Primary source:** European Commission Proposal for a Regulation on Biotechnology, COM(2025)1022, adopted December 16, 2025. Article requires benchtop nucleic acid synthesis devices placed on the EU market to incorporate built-in screening mechanisms for sequences of concern. Currently in ordinary legislative procedure with Parliament and Council; adoption expected 2027.
**Citation:** COM(2025)1022 available at eur-lex.europa.eu; proposal overview: https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/ec1475b7-e3f9-409e-b927-fc7e69306a8c_en
**Cases updated:** SYN-08 (scenario text updated with COM document number and correct date; Precedent Citation updated; proposal-not-law status clarified)

### 5. HHS/OSTP Nucleic Acid Synthesis Screening Framework (April 2024) — Paused Status ✅
**Claim:** Framework paused as of May 2025 under EO.
**Status:** CONFIRMED — paused by EO 14292 before implementation
**Findings:** Framework published April 29, 2024; original implementation date April 29, 2025 for 200 bp screening threshold. Preempted by EO 14292 (May 5, 2025) before taking effect. As of April 2026, replacement guidance has not been published. NIH NOT-OD-25-012 referenced the framework for NIH grant procurement requirements but its status post-EO is uncertain.
**Primary source:** https://bidenwhitehouse.archives.gov/ostp/news-updates/2024/04/29/framework-for-nucleic-acid-synthesis-screening/ (original); EO 14292 (May 5, 2025)
**Cases updated:** SYN-08 (Precedent Citation and Regulatory Anchor updated with confirmed EO number and pause date)
